The Injection Report
Allergan Gutted Allē and Hoped You Wouldn't Notice

What's going on: Allergan Aesthetics rolled out a restructured Allē loyalty program this week, reducing point values by roughly 30% across Botox, Juvéderm, and SkinMedica redemptions. Points that previously converted at $1-per-100 now sit closer to $0.70. The change went live with minimal announcement — a single email buried under a subject line about "exciting updates."

What it means: This is Allergan responding to Galderma's Aspire program eating its market share. But the math punishes loyal patients, not providers. If you've been banking points for a big fill session, your haul just shrank. Injectors are frustrated too — several posted this week that they weren't briefed before the change hit.

What's next: Ask your injector if they're stacking any in-office promotions to offset the Allē haircut. Some practices are already building bridge discounts to keep patients from jumping to Restylane.

If you have Allē points, redeem them now — there's no guarantee the conversion rate won't drop again. Log in, check your balance, and book before end of month.

Put It In Your Mouth
High-Dose Omega-3s Are Coming for Your Nasolabial Folds

What's going on: A randomized controlled trial published this week in the *Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology* found that 12 weeks of high-dose EPA/DHA supplementation (2.4g/day) significantly improved skin elasticity and depth of nasolabial folds compared to placebo — measured by cutometry, not vibes. The effect was strongest in women 35–50.

What that means: This isn't "fish oil is good for you" dressed up. The dose matters. Most over-the-counter omega-3s top out at 1g combined EPA/DHA. You'd need a pharmaceutical-grade or concentrated formula to hit the 2.4g threshold used in the study.

This week's cheat meal: The lobster roll at Luke's, or your regional equivalent — wild-caught, mayo-based, on a split-top bun. Omega-3s you can taste. No lemon needed.

This Week’s Cheat Meal
A proper lobster roll — Luke's or equivalent. Wild-caught, cold prep, mayo not butter, on a split-top bun with nothing to prove.
Look for supplements listing EPA and DHA separately on the label. You want at least 1,400mg EPA and 1,000mg DHA per serving to match the study dose. If the label just says "fish oil 1,000mg," keep walking.

Lip Lab
The Lip Oil Bubble Just Popped (and Clinique Knows It)

What's going on: Clinique launched its Almost Lipstick in three new shades this week — a quiet bet on sheer pigment at a moment when every other brand is still chasing the lip oil gold rush. Meanwhile, NPD data reported this week shows lip oil sales growth flattening for the first time in 18 months.

Why it matters: Lip oils over-promised hydration and under-delivered longevity. The pendulum is swinging back toward tinted balms and sheer lipsticks that actually deposit color. Clinique's formula uses a blend of shea and murumuru butters — emollient, not occlusive — which means it plays well under or over liner.

The verdict: Black Honey remains the icon, but the new shade Tender Heart is the quiet winner — a dusty rose that reads like your lips after a glass of red. Buildable. Doesn't migrate. Your work bag needs it.

If your lip oil collection has quietly become a graveyard of sticky tubes you never finish, this is your permission to pivot back to sheer pigment.
