The Injection Report
Allergan Overhauled Allē and Your Wallet Feels It

What's going on: Allergan Aesthetics rolled out a restructured Allē loyalty program this week, slashing point values and raising redemption thresholds across Botox, Juvéderm, and SkinMedica purchases. Members who'd been banking points toward free syringes are waking up to significantly less spending power — some reporting a 30–40% effective devaluation on social media.

What it means: This tracks with Allergan's post-AbbVie consolidation playbook: tighten margins on the consumer side as patent cliffs loom and biosimilar neuromodulators inch closer to market. For patients, the practical effect is $50–$150 more per filler session depending on volume.

What's next: Ask your injector if they're shifting any volume to Galderma's Aspire program — Restylane and Sculptra rewards just got comparatively more attractive. Some practices are eating the difference to retain patients. Yours might too, but only if you ask.

Before your next appointment, screenshot your current Allē balance and compare it to the new redemption tiers. If your injector offers in-office loyalty stacking, now is when that matters.

Put It In Your Mouth
Teff Is the Collagen-Adjacent Grain You're About to See Everywhere

What's going on: A study published this week in *Nutrients* found that teff — the tiny Ethiopian grain behind injera — contains unusually high levels of lysine and proline, two amino acids that are direct precursors to collagen synthesis. The paper compared teff's amino acid profile to quinoa and amaranth, and teff won on both counts.

What that means: No grain is going to replace your collagen peptide powder, but swapping your morning oats for teff porridge gives you a more complete amino acid base for the synthesis your body's already trying to do. It's also naturally gluten-free and iron-rich, which is relevant if your ferritin's been low.

This week's cheat meal: Bob's Red Mill teff cooked into a thick porridge with tahini, date syrup, and flaky salt. Tastes like halva met oatmeal. No apologies.

Lip Lab
The Merit Lip Liner Restock Already Has a Waitlist

What's going on: Merit's Slip Shade Matte Lipstick in the shade "Baby" restocked Tuesday after selling out in 11 minutes during its initial June drop. This time they added a matching lip liner — Shade Slick in "Bare" — and it's already on backorder.

Why it matters: The formula uses a hydrogenated castor oil base that gives a glossy-matte hybrid finish without the usual wax drag. No fragrance, no lanolin, which matters if your lips react to everything.

The verdict: The liner is the real star. Creamy without being soft enough to smudge, neutral enough to layer under anything. If you run warm, "Bare" might read slightly cool — swatch first if you can.

Sign up for the restock alert on the liner specifically — the lipstick will be easier to grab. Merit drops restocks Tuesdays at 9 AM ET.
