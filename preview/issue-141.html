The Injection Report
Allergan's Allē Overhaul Is Reshuffling the Line

What's going on: Allergan rolled out a revamped Allē loyalty tier system this week, and injectors across the country are reporting chaos. The new structure pushes high-volume patients into priority scheduling windows, effectively creating a two-tier booking system at practices that rely on Allergan products. Multiple providers have posted about it — the consensus is that nobody was consulted.

What it means: If your injector uses Botox, Juvéderm, or SkinVive, their office workflow just changed whether they wanted it or not. Practices that built their schedules around consistent patient flow are now fielding complaints from loyal clients who suddenly can't get their usual slots. It also incentivizes practitioners to push Allergan products over competitors like Daxxify or RHA, which raises its own questions.

What's next: Ask your injector directly whether their booking has changed and whether they're Allergan-exclusive. If they are, ask why.

If your usual appointment slot vanished this month, it's probably not your injector — it's Allergan's new tiering system. Call the office, not the app.

Put It In Your Mouth
The Olive Oil Study That Has Derms Arguing

What's going on: A new cohort study out of the University of Barcelona, published this week in *Nutrients*, found that participants consuming 40+ mL of extra virgin olive oil daily showed measurably improved skin elasticity and hydration markers over 12 weeks compared to controls. The effect was most pronounced in women 35–50. Dermatologists are split — some are calling it the strongest dietary skin evidence in years, others say the cohort was too small and too Mediterranean to generalize.

What that means: Forty mL is about three tablespoons. That's a lot of olive oil, but it's not unreasonable if you're already cooking with it and finishing salads. The polyphenol concentration matters more than the volume — cheap olive oil won't replicate this.

This week's cheat meal: The lobster roll at Saltie Girl, or wherever you are — the drawn butter version with a side of fries you eat with your hands. Summer started, act like it.

What's going on: Patrick Ta dropped the Major Headlines Lip Crème this week — a satin-matte formula in 12 shades that the brand is positioning against the Soft Pinch liquid blush halo.
